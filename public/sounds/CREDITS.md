# Sound credits

Field recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). Every one was trimmed, levelled and,
for the loops, cross-faded into a seamless loop by `scripts/prep-sounds.py`; nothing else was changed.

([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)) and say the sound was edited.
